    Every punch has a tell: a glove flashes white as it winds up. Dodge, duck or block at the last moment, and a missed punch leaves the boxer dazed and open for a few hits. Punches into a raised guard cost you a heart. Run out of hearts and you are too tired to punch until you slip an attack. Hit a boxer who is showing off, or counter certain wind-ups, to earn a star for a heavy uppercut. Three knockdowns in a round ends the fight. If you go down, hammer the punch buttons to get up.
