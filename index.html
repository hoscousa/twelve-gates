Punch, punch, punch ends in an uppercut. Punch, punch, kick ends in a roundhouse. Landing hits fills the gold Chi bar. A helicopter kick (Spin), rising fist (Rise) or fireball (Fire) spends a quarter of it. Hold Fire to charge a great fireball that costs half the bar and goes through everyone. A dragon kick (Chi) needs the whole bar and cannot be blocked. Fighters carrying nunchaku, a pole or a monk staff drop them when knocked out. Walk over one to pick it up, swing it with Punch, or throw it. After a kick or a couple of punches stagger an enemy, press Throw to grab them and hurl them into the others. Hold a direction to choose which way. If you get knocked down, press Kick for a backflip bicycle kick. On a phone, thumb buttons appear under the fight. Turn it sideways for two players.
